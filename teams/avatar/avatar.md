# 🧩 Avatar Generation Prompt Guide  
### Style Reference for WhatsApp Group Avatars — "AI Arena" Series  

This collection defines the visual and thematic style for anthropomorphic animal avatars representing different regional species and personality archetypes.  
Each avatar should follow **the same visual grammar** as the *Jaguar* and *Toucan* reference images, ensuring stylistic coherence across all teams.

---

## 🎨 General Art Direction
- **Format:** Square composition (1:1), ideal for WhatsApp profile icons.  
- **Pose:** Frontal, torso view, confident and centered.  
- **Lighting:** Warm and balanced, cinematic but soft.  
- **Environment:** The **AI Arena** — a circular stone platform surrounded by glowing blue crystal pillars under a translucent energy dome. Slight depth-of-field blur for focus.  
- **Art style:** Semi-realistic digital painting with fantasy tones; armor and attire inspired by light sci-fi or medieval tech design.  
- **Armor design:** Lightweight metallic armor with **glowing green elements**, usually on chest, shoulders, and accessories.  
- **Symbolism:** Each character bears a **luminescent emblem** on its chest representing its theme (technology, knowledge, empathy, creativity, etc.).  
- **Mood:** Heroic, calm, and intelligent — designed to look trustworthy and capable.  
- **Color palette:** Emerald greens, turquoise blues, and amber sands.  
- **Consistency:** Match proportions, shading, and background treatment from the *Jaguar* and *Toucan* avatars.
